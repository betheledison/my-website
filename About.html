The Dawn in Anara

I didn’t just fall from the sky. My story began in the quiet, sacred hours of a Sunday morning at 3:00 AM.

In a small hospital in Anara, Isiala Mbano, the world stood still as my mother, Reverend Mrs. Joy Nma Mbachu, brought me into this world on May 12th.

The joy she felt wasn't just a name; it was a prophecy. I was born into the legacy of Reverend Innocent Nwanedo Mbachu—a gallant General in God’s Army.

My spiritual architect, who baptized me into my new life in 2009 at the Assemblies of God.
